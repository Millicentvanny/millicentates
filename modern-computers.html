HISTORY OF COMPUTERS

A brief history of the evolution of computers

Electronic Milestones

ENIAC (1945)

The Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer (ENIAC) was one of the earliest electronic general-purpose computers. It was designed to calculate artillery firing tables for the United States Army during World War II. ENIAC used vacuum tubes and could perform thousands of calculations per second.

Harvard Mark I (1944)

The Harvard Mark I, also known as the IBM Automatic Sequence-Controlled Calculator, was one of the first large-scale automatic digital computers. It was built by Howard Aiken at Harvard University and was used for various scientific calculations.

UNIVAC I (1951)

The UNIVAC I (Universal Automatic Computer I) was the first commercially produced computer in the United States. It was designed for business and government applications and was capable of processing large amounts of data quickly.

Microprocessors (1971)

The microprocessor, invented by Intel in 1971, revolutionized the computer industry by integrating the entire central processing unit onto a single chip. This innovation led to the development of personal computers and made computing more accessible to individuals and businesses.
