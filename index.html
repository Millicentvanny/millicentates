HISTORY OF COMPUTERS

A brief history of the evolution of computers

Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Trace the development of computers from early mechanical devices to modern electronic systems
  • Identify the importance of pioneers in computer history and explain their contributions
  • Describe the major milestones in the development of electronic computers including the Harvard Mark I, ENIAC, EDVAC, and the microprocessor
  • to explain how computers benefits student, workers and the society in daily life
